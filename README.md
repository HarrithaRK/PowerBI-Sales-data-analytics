# 📊 Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product demand, discounts, and order details.

The dashboard enables users to identify top- and bottom-performing products, analyze sales trends over different time periods, compare business performance between selected periods, and explore order-level information using interactive visual filters.

The primary objective is to transform sales data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Identify the top and bottom five products based on sales, profit, and quantity sold.
- Analyze sales trends daily, monthly, quarterly, and annually.
- Understand the relationship between sales and profit.
- Compare sales, profit, and quantity sold between two user-selected periods.
- Calculate the average discount offered in each discount category.
- Display the total number of orders.
- Analyze sales, profit, discounts, net sales, and other available order-level details.
- Enable interactive filtering by product, date, customer ID, and promotion category.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Top and Bottom 5 Products

Identify the five highest-performing and five lowest-performing products based on:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Quantity Sold

This analysis helps identify successful products and products that may require further investigation.

### 2. Sales Trend Analysis

Analyze sales performance across different time intervals:

- Daily Sales
- Monthly Sales
- Quarterly Sales
- Annual Sales

This feature helps users understand sales patterns, monitor changes over time, and identify periods of higher or lower sales.

### 3. Relationship Between Sales and Profit

Analyze the relationship between sales and profit to understand how sales performance corresponds to profitability.

This analysis can help identify products or periods with high sales but comparatively low profits.

### 4. Period Comparison Analysis

Allow users to select two periods and compare their performance using:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity Sold

This feature supports flexible comparisons between selected dates or time ranges and helps users understand changes in business performance.

### 5. Average Discount by Discount Category

Calculate and compare the average discount offered across different discount categories.

This helps users understand discount distribution and investigate how discounting varies between categories.

### 6. Total Number of Orders

Display the total number of orders as a key business metric.

This provides an overview of order volume and helps users monitor overall transaction activity.

### 7. Detailed Order Analysis with Interactive Filters

Provide detailed order-level information, including:

- Sales
- Profit
- Discount
- Net Sales
- Other available order fields

Users can explore the data using visual filters such as:

- Product
- Date
- Customer ID
- Promotion Category

These filters enable users to investigate specific products, customers, promotional categories, and time periods.

## 📈 Business Questions Answered

The dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions:

1. Which five products generate the highest sales?
2. Which five products generate the lowest sales?
3. Which five products generate the highest and lowest profits?
4. Which products have the highest and lowest quantities sold?
5. How do sales trends change daily, monthly, quarterly, and annually?
6. What relationship exists between sales and profit?
7. How do sales, profit, and quantity sold differ between two selected periods?
8. What is the average discount offered in each discount category?
9. What is the total number of orders?
10. How do sales, profit, discounts, and net sales vary across products, dates, customers, and promotion categories?

## 🖥️ How to Use the Dashboard

1. Open the Power BI report in Power BI Desktop.
2. Explore the sales, profit, quantity sold, and order-count information.
3. Review the top and bottom five products for each performance metric.
4. Analyze sales trends across daily, monthly, quarterly, and annual periods.
5. Select two periods to compare sales, profit, and quantity sold.
6. Review average discounts across discount categories.
7. Use the available visual filters to explore order-level details by product, date, customer ID, and promotion category.
8. Clear or modify filters to explore different segments of the dataset.
